    Policies & FAQ

    Clear terms, before you travel.

    Still unsure about something? Call +91 81108 49375 or WhatsApp us — a real person answers.

    Is my trip booked once I receive the itinerary?
    No. The itinerary is a proposal, and no bookings are made until you confirm it. Everything in it is subject to availability, so we recommend confirming early to avoid last-minute hassles. Your booking is confirmed with the 30% advance payment.
    How do payments work?
    In three parts:
    • 30% advance when you sign the contract or confirm the booking.
    • 50% ten days before the departure date.
    • The final 20% on day 1 of the tour, when you reach the resort.
    What happens if I cancel?
    It depends on how close to departure you cancel:
    • 30 days or more before: a 10% administrative deduction on the total trip cost.
    • 15–29 days before: a 30% retention fee.
    • 0–14 days before: a 100% retention fee, because of resort block-booking rules.
    What is not included in the trip cost?
    Anything not mentioned in your itinerary — for example personal expenses, food beyond what's listed, early check-in or late checkout, and camera or video permits at sights. Some charges are paid directly at the destination, such as entry tickets, vehicle entry or green tax at hill stations and sanctuaries, and optional activities like boating, jeep safaris and paragliding. Your itinerary lists exactly what is included and excluded.
    What if the hotel in my itinerary isn't available?
    Hotels are subject to availability. If the one proposed is not available, we provide similar rooms.
    Can sightseeing days and timings change?
    Yes. The itinerary is a guideline for the tour, so the days and timings of sightseeing may change on the ground.
    Do you plan trips outside South India?
    Yes — Goa, Jaipur and Kullu Manali within India, the Andaman Islands, and international trips to Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Thailand and Singapore. Tell us your dates, budget and group size and we'll send an itinerary.
    How do you use my personal details?
    Only to plan and run your trip — we share them with the hotels, drivers, guides and permit authorities involved, and we never sell them. Read the full privacy policy.
